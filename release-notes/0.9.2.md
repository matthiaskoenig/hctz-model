# Release notes for hctz-model 0.9.2

Made from pkdb_models 8653b01de0 with pkpdsim 0.9.4.

The changes since 0.9.1.

## Core libraries

pkpdsim 0.9.2 → 0.9.4:

- `PKPDSimulationExperiment` does not declare `Mr`, the molecular weights of the substances.
- A curve over the values of a dimension of its scan (`x = <dimension>.<target>`, e.g. the net sodium at 12 hr over the scanned renal function of HCTZ Knauf1995 Fig3) becomes the band of the quantiles of the draws with the median, as a curve over the time.
- A curve over another variable of its task, which differs between the draws (e.g. the blood pressure over the HCTZ concentration of the dose dependency), has no common x for the quantiles of a band.
